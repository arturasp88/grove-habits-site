A minute a day.

Open it, tick what you did, watch it land. These are the app's own screens.

  1. Step 1

    Choose what to keep

    Yes-or-no habits, counts to a target, or limits to stay under. Every day, on set days, or a few times a week.

  2. Step 2

    Tick it off

    One tap. Part of the way still counts: three glasses of eight is three glasses grown.

  3. Step 3

    Watch it land

    Each habit plants its own thing on today's ground: a cherry tree, a pond, a bench. The day closes and the land is yours.

An island, not a streak chart.

Five things it does that a row of ticks cannot.

  • Every habit plants its own thing.

    You choose what each habit grows and the colour of its flowers. Look across the island and you can see which habits made it.

  • A missed day is a quiet meadow.

    Never a punishment. Nothing you have grown is ever taken away, and you can put yesterday right the next morning.

  • Keep at it and a monument goes up.

    72150100

    At 7, 21 and 50 days, what a habit plants grows a size bigger. At each of those, and at 100, it raises a monument.

  • Your habits build a village.

    Keeping habits earns the coin the island runs on. Spend it on sixteen buildings, from a campfire to a beacon tower.

  • A day is worth the same, however long your list.

    Each day shares one fixed amount of growth between your habits. Ten small ones earn no more than three real ones, so there is nothing to gain by padding the list.

Questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature and every habit is free, in the Grove world. Mars, Moon, Empire and Frontier are optional, bought once, and only change how the island looks.

When is it out?

Soon, on Google Play. There is no date yet. The first twelve get in before anyone else, and everyone on the list gets one email the day it is out.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at present. Grove Habits is made for Android phones.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Your habits are kept on your phone and we cannot see them.

What happens when I miss a day?

That day's land stays a quiet meadow. Nothing you have grown is taken away, and you can put yesterday right the next morning.

What do the first twelve have to do?

Install the test version from Google Play, use it as you normally would for a fortnight, and tell us what works and what does not.

The first twelve get every world.

Twelve places in the closed test on Google Play. Each comes with a code that unlocks Mars, Moon, Empire and Frontier, free, for good.

Use the email of the Google account on your Android phone: that is the address Google Play will let in. You get one email with your invitation and your code, and one when the app is out. Nothing else.
